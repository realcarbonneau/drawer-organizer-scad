MODULAR DRAWER ORGANIZER v3 — OpenSCAD 2021.01

1. Keep modular-organizer-v3.scad and modular-organizer-v3.json in the same folder.
2. In Customizer choose Presets > Kitchen cutlery drawer (one preset only).
3. Drawer panel: sets physical profile, layout columns, front/rear depths.
4. Tray panel: choose Output > Full current drawer layout to inspect the entire set.
5. Choose Output > One selected tray, then Part type to export one tray.
6. Connectors panel: settings are independent of loose connector quantity.

DEFAULT LAYOUT: 1 front full-width, 4 middle columns, 1 rear full-width.
Top inside width 218 mm, flat floor width 190 mm, bottom radius 10 mm,
depth 475 mm, metal rim height 65 mm, tray 10 mm higher than rim.
45-degree exterior chamfer, auto-calculated inside fillet for base thickness.

NOTE: The front and rear outer metal wall profile and lip fit are still
provisional. Test-print a short cross-sectional sample before committing to
full-height parts. "Print half" is inherited from the author's original design
and exports one half rather than automatically producing both.
